About us

Building stronger partnerships for a sustainable tomorrow

RAK NovaChem is an industrial chemical partner based in Pune. We combine engineering, sourcing and commercial experience to deliver the right chemical, to specification, on time.

Who we are

Quality you can trust. Partnership you can rely on.

RAK NovaChem is a trusted industrial chemical partner, committed to providing high-purity chemicals and reliable supply solutions for a wide range of industries.

With a focus on quality, consistency and customer satisfaction, we bridge global chemical technologies with local market needs, from water treatment plants and construction sites to paint, polymer, textile and agro manufacturers.

Our vision

To be a preferred and reliable chemical partner, known for quality, innovation and a customer-centric approach.

Our mission

To deliver high-quality chemicals and value-added solutions with integrity, efficiency and long-term partnership.
